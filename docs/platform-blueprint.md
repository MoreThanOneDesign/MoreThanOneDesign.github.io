# MoreThanOne Platform Blueprint

## 1. Platform Vision

MoreThanOne Platform  is a custom business, website and e-commerce management systen for MoreThanOneDesign.

The platform must allow owners and employees to manage the complete business without editing code.

## 2. Core Principles

- Easy to understand without a manual
- Secure individual user accounts
- Roles and custom permissions
- Consistent MoreThanOneDesign styling
- Expandable with new modules
- Mobile and tablet friendly
- Everything reachable within three clicks
- Draft, review, published and archived workflows
- Activity logging
- Recycle bin for deleted content
